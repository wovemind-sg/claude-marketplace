## Who it applies to and thresholds

| Scope point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Role under the EU AI Act | Calling a third-party model API (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, via OpenRouter/LiteLLM/LangChain) and putting it in your product = **deployer**, and usually **provider of an AI system** (your app) for Art. 50 purposes. You are a GPAI model provider only if you train/fine-tune and place a model on the market under your name (substantial fine-tuning of a GPAI model can make you one — Needs a lawyer). |
| Territorial | AI Act applies when the AI system is placed on the EU market or its output is used in the EU (Art. 2(1)), regardless of company seat. No SME exemption from Art. 50; SMEs get lower fine caps and simplified documentation. |
| Risk tiers | Prohibited practices (Art. 5, since 2 Feb 2025) → high-risk (Annex III: biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, essential services incl. credit/insurance, law enforcement, migration, justice) → transparency duties (Art. 50) → minimal risk. Typical micro-SaaS chat/summarise/generate features = Art. 50 only. |
| Omnibus status | AI "Digital Omnibus" provisionally agreed 7 May 2026: Annex III high-risk obligations postponed (to 2 Dec 2027 per agreement), Art. 50(2) marking gets a grace period to 2 Dec 2026 for systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026; Art. 50(1) unaffected — confirm Official Journal publication via source. |
| Penalties | Art. 50 breaches: up to €15M or 3% of worldwide turnover (lower of the two for SMEs, Art. 99(6)). |
| US | No federal AI statute. Colorado SB 26-189 (signed 14 May 2026) repealed and replaced SB 24-205; replacement effective 1 Jan 2027, mainly notice + human-review duties for consequential automated decisions — confirm via source. Utah AI Policy Act (disclosure on request; proactive for regulated occupations). California BPC §17941 bot disclosure; CA SB 243 companion-chatbot law (from 1 Jan 2026) — confirm via source. |

| Label deepfakes and AI text published on matters of public interest | AI Act Art. 50(4) (deployer duty) | Features generating realistic people/voices or publishing articles | Feature file:line | Required when feature exists | founder | Visible "AI-generated" label on such outputs. |

## Needs a lawyer
- Any feature touching Annex III areas (hiring, credit, education, biometrics, insurance) — high-risk classification.
- Whether fine-tuning or distilling a model makes the company a GPAI provider.
- Lawful basis and transfer assessment for sending special-category or children's data to model providers.
- Ownership and infringement risk of AI-generated output offered to customers.
- California companion-chatbot (SB 243) and Colorado consequential-decision duties if those features exist.
- Whether in-product disclosures satisfy Utah and California bot-disclosure laws for the actual user journeys.
- Negotiating zero-data-retention or EU-residency terms with model vendors for sensitive deployments.
